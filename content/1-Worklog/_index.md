### Introduction

This worklog records my AWS learning experience and project activities during the **First Cloud Journey** program, following a 12-week schedule from September 14, 2026, to December 5, 2026.

During the program, I learned about AWS services such as EC2, S3, VPC, RDS, Lambda, API Gateway, and others. Most of my learning involved reading documentation, reviewing examples, and completing basic exercises. I also paid attention to AWS costs.

Besides learning AWS, I worked on **LoveSpot**, a restaurant recommendation and itinerary planning application for Ho Chi Minh City. I started by learning about the project requirements and main features. Later, I worked on venue data, the recommendation system, and feature testing.

In the final weeks, I spent more time on LoveSpot. By Week 11, the application had been deployed to AWS, and the architecture diagram and demo video had been completed. Week 12 focuses on reviewing AWS knowledge and summarizing the work.
