### Introduction

This worklog documents my AWS learning journey and project development activities during the **First Cloud Journey** program, covering 12 weeks from September 14, 2026, to December 5, 2026.

Throughout the program, I started with fundamental AWS services such as Amazon EC2, S3, and VPC, then continued exploring Database, Serverless, Networking, Monitoring, Security, and other cloud services. My learning activities mainly involved official documentation, tutorials, and basic hands-on exercises. I also focused on resource management, cost control, and avoiding unnecessary charges from AWS services.

Alongside my AWS studies, during **Weeks 10, 11, and 12**, I allocated most of my time to developing a **restaurant recommendation and itinerary planning application for Ho Chi Minh City**. The project focuses on processing restaurant and location data, building a recommendation system based on user preferences, integrating the Frontend and Backend, and testing the generation of suitable dining itineraries.

For each of the final three weeks, the plan includes **2 days of AWS learning and 4 days of project work**. These activities are documented to track learning progress, application development, challenges encountered, and areas for improvement throughout each stage.
